Notepads fixed mathematical font assets
=========================================

The 20 KaTeX TTF files in fonts/ are unmodified byte copies from the fixed
RaTeX revision 5f7dc4883aba8eeb30fe34f8a104923f43f734bf. Their exact byte
lengths, upstream URLs and SHA256 digests are recorded in ASSETS.json.
The original local research input manifest is also bound by SHA256 there.

These fonts are licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1, not the project's
MIT license. Their original copyright and Reserved Font Names remain in
the font metadata. Keep licenses/SIL-OFL-1.1.txt and the original notices
with distributions that embed the fonts. The four files in licenses/ are
preserved without edits; references to upstream repository directories in
those original notices describe the upstream distribution.

The fixed asset table includes Caligraphic-Bold to preserve the original
asset set. RaTeX 0.1.15 has no FontId/metrics entry for that face; its
presence does not claim layout support or create a substitute metric.

See licenses/KaTeX-fonts-NOTICE.txt, licenses/KaTeX-LICENSE.txt, and
licenses/RaTeX-THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt for original attribution.
